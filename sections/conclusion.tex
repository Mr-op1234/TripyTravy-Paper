\section{Conclusion}
The Creator exports a passphrase-protected snapshot, and the Viewer verifies it before saving the itinerary for offline use. Host tests confirmed legacy migration and protected import. A newer revision can replace the schedule while preserving the traveler's checklist and completion status, and repeated attachments took less space in the controlled fixtures. The Windows and Android builds can now be used to test phone delivery, airplane-mode access, and recovery from an interrupted import.
